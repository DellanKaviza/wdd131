Nathy Haven Nail Studio

Website Planning Document

Site Name

Nathy Haven Nail Studio

This name was chosen because it reflects both the business owner (Nathy) and the idea of a relaxing “haven” where clients can enjoy professional nail care and feel comfortable.

Site Purpose

The purpose of this website is to promote Nathy Haven Nail Studio by providing information about nail services, displaying past work in a gallery, sharing pricing, and allowing clients to contact or book appointments.

Scenarios

  • What nail services does Nathy Haven offer and how much do they cost?
  • How can I book an appointment or contact the nail technician?

Color Scheme

  • Pink (#d63384): Used for headings, highlights, and accents.
  • Light Pink (#fff0f5): Used as the background color.
  • White (#ffffff): Used for content sections.

Typography

  • Arial: Used for body text for readability.
  • Headings: Arial bold style for titles and section headings.
